Privacy Policy — DairyBook

App: DairyBook – Daily Money Tracker Developer / Publisher: SMSoftMart (Registered Proprietary Firm, India) Contact: support@smsoftmart.com Website: www.smsoftmart.com Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This policy explains what DairyBook collects, why, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Using DairyBook without an account

You can use DairyBook without creating an account or signing in. When you use the app this way, everything you enter (transactions, budgets, GST invoices, bill splits and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive or store that data on our servers.

2. Optional sign-in and cloud sync

DairyBook offers an optional sign-in (email and password, mobile number via OTP, or Google) so your data can be backed up and synced across your devices. If, and only if, you choose to sign in:

  • We collect the account identifier you sign in with (your email address, or mobile number, or the basic Google account profile you approve).
  • The financial records you create in the app (transactions, budgets, invoices and bill splits) are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under your account, so they can be restored and kept in sync.

Authentication and cloud storage are provided by Google Firebase. If you never sign in, none of this data leaves your device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DairyBook shows ads to free users through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising identifier and standard technical information. DairyBook Pro subscribers see no ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device Settings, under Google → Ads.

4. No SMS or message access

DairyBook does not read your SMS or messages and requests no SMS permission. If you want to import a bank or UPI transaction, you copy the message text and paste it into the app yourself; the app parses the pasted text on your device. Nothing is read from your inbox automatically.

5. In-app purchases

The optional DairyBook Pro subscription is processed by Google Play Billing. Payment is handled by Google; SMSoftMart never receives or stores your card or payment details.

6. Affiliate links

The “Earn More” section contains affiliate/referral links to third-party partners. If you tap them and sign up, SMSoftMart may earn a referral commission. We do not share your DairyBook data with these partners; any information you provide is provided directly to that partner under their own policy.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the features above: Google Firebase (authentication and cloud sync, only if you sign in), Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Billing (purchases). Each processes data under Google’s policies.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately via Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Cloud data (only if you signed in): you can request deletion of your account and its synced data at any time by emailing support@smsoftmart.com; we will delete it within 30 days.

9. Children

DairyBook is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Security

Data sent to Firebase and AdMob is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The “Last Updated” date above reflects the current version.
